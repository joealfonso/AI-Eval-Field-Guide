## Agency — surface, don't decide (—)
- [—] The system presents options, evidence, or analysis rather than silently executing irreversible actions on the user's behalf.
- [—] A human can see what the AI is about to do before it happens, not only after.
- [—] High-stakes actions (financial, legal, personal data, compliance, employment) always route through explicit human confirmation.

## Transparency — reasoning is legible (—)
- [—] A confidence level or certainty signal is shown, not just a final answer.
- [—] The reasoning behind a suggestion is visible in plain language, not buried in a tooltip or absent entirely.
- [—] A user can trace an output back to the source data or logic that produced it.

## Honesty — limits are disclosed (—)
- [—] The system has an explicit "can't determine this" state, rather than guessing with false confidence.
- [—] Edge cases and low-confidence outputs are flagged distinctly from high-confidence ones, not blended together in the same visual treatment.
- [—] Product or marketing copy around this feature doesn't oversell what the AI can reliably do.

## Equity — works across different users (—)
- [—] The design was tested with assistive technology (screen reader, keyboard-only, voice control), not just visually reviewed.
- [—] The system accounts for different user contexts (expert vs. novice, high-stakes vs. routine) rather than a single path for everyone.
- [—] Output has been checked for systematic bias against any user group, language, region, or edge-case identity.

## Real reduction — not hidden complexity (—)
- [—] The AI measurably reduces steps, tools, or time, not just visual clutter while the underlying complexity remains.
- [—] Automation is reversible, a user can undo, edit, or override without starting the whole task over.
- [—] The feature has been validated with real task-timing or usage data, not only a demo or stakeholder walkthrough.

## Failure design — graceful degradation (—)
- [—] There's a clear, designed path for what happens when the AI is wrong, not just a generic error state.
- [—] Unfixable or ambiguous cases are flagged explicitly and don't silently block or corrupt unrelated work.
- [—] A human reviewer can always intervene without needing engineering support to do it.
