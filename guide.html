Guide

Guide to AI evaluation

The patterns are the core of this site. The guide is the order to read everything else in: four short chapters, starting with a worked example, then question lists for the moment you are in, then reference pages.

Short on time? Pick a role under Start here on the home page, or go straight to Use it now for questions you can ask today.

How this guide works

This guide is built around three moves. Choose a claim: write down exactly what is being claimed and what decision depends on it. Test it: find the patterns that apply and ask their questions, in order, of the evidence behind the claim. Decide what you can conclude and what you still cannot, and say both. The walkthrough does all three slowly, on three real published cases.
