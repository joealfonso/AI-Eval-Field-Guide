Changelog

What changed on this site and when. Corrections and revisions to a pattern also appear in that pattern's revision history.

  1. 19 Oct 2026

    The AI vendor questionnaire template now has four parts and 34 questions. Part A is the original 17 evidence questions. New parts cover contract and operations, data handling, and security and misuse; these are this guide’s own questions, not drawn from the patterns. It adds a 0 to 2 scoring scheme that reports each part separately instead of one total, a list of which questions to ask at which risk tier, fallbacks for when a vendor will not answer, and a summary sheet. Templates: AI vendor questionnaire

  2. 19 Oct 2026

    Wording corrections after an independent content review. The GSM1k correlation is now stated as the paper reports it (a Spearman statistic of 0.36, written as r² in its abstract). The Goodhart’s Law page now says the 27 Meta variants were tested in March 2025 alone. The paired-comparison rule on the No Error Bars page matches the sample-size page (about 3% to 4% disagreement). Wong et al. (2021) is removed from the Distribution Shift page, which had called it not a clean case, and the Clark et al. (2021) point on the Gold Standard Myth page now says it concerns spotting machine text. Methodology: how the evidence was checked

  3. 19 Oct 2026

    Reading aids and a clearer line between the research and this guide’s own framework. Every pattern page now opens with a one-sentence bottom line and its main caveat, which the Share button copies together with the link. A new walkthrough, “A claim, read slowly”, is now chapter 1 of the Guide: it takes three real cases through choosing the claim, testing it and deciding. The About page has a new section on what comes from the research and what is this guide’s own, plus a trust block, and “this guide’s own” markers run through the long-form pages. The Evidence base labels have clearer names. Three patterns have reworded aphorisms, and the rule of thumb for prompt variants, repeated runs and labels is now consistent across pages. A claim, read slowly About: research vs this guide’s own

  4. 19 Oct 2026

    Full accuracy re-audit. Every claim on the pattern pages, guide pages, glossary, bibliography, case studies and tools was checked against its primary source. It found five factual errors (a misquoted sentence, a wrong author name, a wrong statistical rule, two experiments reported as one, and a measure described as something it was not) and several dozen statements that were stronger than their sources or missing a caveat. All were corrected, softened or removed, and every changed pattern is now v1.1 with a note in its revision history. The bibliography now counts each work once, the Evidence base and name labels were reviewed (Criteria Drift is now a recent term from one paper, and Functionality Fallacy is a name used here), and the Readiness Review has a scope note on the EU AI Act and NIST references. Methodology About: evidence base

  5. 19 Oct 2026

    Review pass. New home page with a Start here block and a clearer Guide hub (the overview essay moved to its own page). Added Sample size and error bars, Real cases, and Templates. Pattern pages now show an Evidence base label and a shorter header on phones. The claim checker has a tested rule set and says plainly when nothing matches. The checklist groups questions by phase and scales to the stakes. Share links now use ?patterns= (old ?laws= links still work). Privacy page now spells out Google Analytics use. Guide Sample size Real cases Templates
